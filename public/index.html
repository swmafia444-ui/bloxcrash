FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I deposit?

Click the "Deposit" button and follow the instructions to join the private server and trade with the official bot.

How are winners determined?

Winners are determined by a provably fair coin flip using a secret seed and EOS block hash.

What happens if I disconnect during a match?

The match will still resolve automatically. Your pets will be returned or awarded based on the outcome.

How do I get my pets back after a match?

If you lose, the pets go to the winner. If you win, you receive the opponent's pets. Cancelled matches return your pets.
